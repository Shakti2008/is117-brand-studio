What to review

  • Readability: open counters and substantial strokes keep ASCEND legible at the small size above.
  • Silhouette: a clipped S, squared C, diagonal N, and pointed D establish a consistent angular rhythm.
  • Ascending detail: the A’s inner opening rises toward the right; it is deliberately subtle rather than a separate arrow.
  • Contrast: the same vector paths work in solid black or white. No gradients, filters, or embedded fonts.

This is an original proposal, not an approved identity or a claim of trademark clearance. It has not been applied to the storefront or style guide. The geometric styling is an interpretation of ASCEND’s selected direction, not a reproduction of a museum reference.
