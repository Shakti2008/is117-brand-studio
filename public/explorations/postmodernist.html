Postmodernist direction / Reference and rationale

Commercial quotation. A layered identity.

Reference: Roy Lichtenstein, Drowning Girl (1963).

Direct image inspection: a cropped face and hand sit among thick wave contours, dotted color fields, and a large thought balloon.

  • A dotted background and strong frame quote the visual language of commercial comic printing without reproducing the painting.
  • A white headline panel, offset red shadow, and angled background panel layer different graphic registers while leaving text horizontal.
  • Sans-serif display lettering and monospace support deliberately mix typographic languages within ASCEND's shared palette.

Intended audience effect: Hypothesis: familiar print references and layered framing may support originality and memorability.

Risk: Comic associations and layering may feel playful or busy rather than premium. Dots stay behind the image, away from reading text.

Historical distinction: The reference is Pop Art. This preview explores a postmodern strategy through quotation, layering, and mixing visual languages; Pop Art and postmodernism are not interchangeable labels.
